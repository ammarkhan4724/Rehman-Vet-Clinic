About RLAH

Round Lake Animal Hospital offers a wide range of medical, surgical, and dental services for animals, including wellness exams, vaccinations, spay and neuter procedures, and emergency care. Their experienced and compassionate team of veterinarians and staff are dedicated to providing high-quality and personalized care to each animal and their family. They also provide boarding and grooming services for animals, as well as prescription diets and medications. The hospital is equipped with advanced diagnostic and therapeutic equipment to ensure the best possible care for their patients.
